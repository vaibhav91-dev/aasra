# Aasra presentation journey

Positioning: A multilingual AI companion that helps gig workers understand their earnings and take their next step.

## Preparation

Open https://aasra-one.vercel.app and create a presenter account through Account & backup. Use a unique password. Use only clearly identified synthetic demonstration facts; do not paste real worker identifiers. AI generation is real and requires provider availability.

## Connected demonstration (about three minutes)

1. Sign in. In Earnings, record today's example income of ₹850 and expense of ₹100. Show the calculated net of ₹750. Explain that this represents recorded entries only. Wait for the account save status before moving on.
2. Open Document Help. Paste this clearly fictional notice: “DEMO NOTICE: Example Delivery platform. Payment for 17 October 2026: gross ₹850, deduction ₹100. Reason for deduction not provided. Please contact support for clarification.” Generate an explanation. Point out that Aasra identifies missing information without asserting that the deduction is illegal. Do not add this notice's deduction as a second expense automatically.
3. Choose Draft a grievance about this. In the transfer review, enter only the relevant facts from the notice. Review the prefilled description. Enter Example Delivery as the platform, select deduction, use the notice date and ₹100 disputed amount, and request a reason and review. Confirm the facts and generate. Show that the draft remains editable and nothing is sent externally.
4. Wait for Saved in the account strip. Refresh. Show restored earnings and grievance facts/draft. Document source text intentionally clears. Open AI Help, send a question, wait for its reply and Saved, and refresh to show the completed conversation is restored too. Never claim unsent chat input is saved.
5. Return to Overview. Show Workplace safety, Know my rights and Find welfare schemes. Enter Punjab and Delivery partner, choose welfare, review the prefilled question and send it. Show official myScheme/e-Shram starting links, location context and the eligibility caveat. Without location, demonstrate the assistant asking before personalising guidance.

## Boundaries to explain

The official source catalog is reviewed, not a live scheme search. It supports scheme discovery and verification steps, not verified eligibility, benefit amounts or legal conclusions. Confirm current scheme details with the relevant official authority. Guest chat and grievance text are session-only; sign-in enables account persistence. Password recovery email is not configured. Browser speech input may be available in supported browsers; read-aloud is not implemented.
